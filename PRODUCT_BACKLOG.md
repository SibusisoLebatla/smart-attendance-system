**📝 Justification for Prioritization:**  
- **Must-have stories** are **critical for core system functionality** (e.g., attendance tracking).  
- **Should-have stories** enhance usability (e.g., notifications, history tracking).  
- **Could-have stories** improve convenience (e.g., exporting reports).  
- **Won’t-have stories** (not included here) could be advanced analytics or integrations.
